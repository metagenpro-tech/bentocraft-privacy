Privacy Policy - BentoCraft Pro

Last updated: October 2026

✓ Zero Data Collection Guarantee

1. Overview & Single Purpose

BentoCraft Pro is a browser extension developed to generate Bento Grid design layouts, color palettes, and vector export files (Adobe Illustrator JSX, SVG, PNG, and HTML/CSS) for digital designers and developers.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

BentoCraft Pro adheres strictly to a zero-telemetry, offline-first philosophy:

  • We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email, IP address, or device ID.
  • We do NOT track your web browsing activity, tabs, or web history.
  • We do NOT use third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or remote telemetry servers.
  • We do NOT sell, trade, or transfer any user data to third parties.

3. Zero Permissions Policy

BentoCraft Pro requests zero special browser permissions:

  • No permissions are declared in the extension manifest. It operates completely sandboxed without access to your tabs, web history, or network requests.
  • Local preferences, deterministic layout seed bookmarks, and custom palettes are stored exclusively on your device via standard offline localStorage. No data ever leaves your computer.

4. External Links

The extension contains optional links to external services: a Gumroad creator support page (Buy Me a Coffee) and mailto support links. When you click these external links, you interact directly with those services under their respective privacy policies.
